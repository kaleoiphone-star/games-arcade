DEBRIS DYNASTY:SIMULATION INITIATION

  1. 01

    AIM & LAUNCH

    Move your mouse to position the holographic trajectory line. Click or press SPACE to launch your marble into the debris field.

  2. 02

    COMBO SCALING

    Every peg hit increases your active turn combo multiplier. Rack up continuous bounces to maximize your score! Launch score = sum of peg values × total hits.

  3. 03

    CARD MODULES

    Select a Trigger Card from your bottom dock (or press 1 2 3) before launching to empower your marble with special physics (e.g., Splitting, Lasers, Heavy Mass). Clear stages to unlock new launch gear, MK II upgrades and perks.

  4. 04

    DEFEAT THE BOSS

    Direct your launches to shatter protective peg barriers and strike the Central Scrap-Goliath core before running out of launches. Bosses strike back between shots — the NEXT ▸ warning above each boss shows its next move. Avoid cursed pegs ☠: hitting them heals the boss. Land a marble in the Recovery Bay for a free launch. Between stages, pick your route on the map — shops, salvage caches, recharge docks or elite bosses — and collect relics that combo with your cards. Survive all ten stages to secure the Dynasty. Your run is saved at the start of every stage.
